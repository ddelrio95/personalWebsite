About

A Simple Obsession

I'm an engineer with one guiding instinct: take things that are messy, complicated, or overwhelming and make them clear. It's the thread that runs through everything I build.

I'm endlessly curious and happiest when I'm building — always tinkering with a new idea and chasing the moment a hard problem finally clicks into place. I care as much about the people the work is for as the work itself.

Beyond building software, I'm drawn to mergers & acquisitions — the strategy of buying, improving, and scaling businesses. It's the same instinct in a different arena: taking something with potential and making it work better.
